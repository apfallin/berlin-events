    Sources *

    📱 Gigs — the Berlin-Gigs community pad on Cryptpad, a DIY community list of concerts with time, price, genre, smoke-free and wheelchair info. Covers Sep–Dec 2026.

    🎸 MTI — mytrueintent.blogspot.com, a Berlin indie/rock concert blog. Imported from its spreadsheet export: ~2,100 shows, Oct 2026 – May 2027, with notes (sold out, postponed, cancelled — excluded).

    🎪 Mob — mobilize.berlin, a Berlin Mobilizon instance: federated DIY platform for concerts, parties and community events.

    🌐 Web — curated picks from official sites and venues; each event links directly to its source page.

    Events appearing in more than one source are automatically deduplicated (same title + date) and marked with all matching source badges.

    ⚠️ This is an experimental site, a constant work in progress — it is by no means exhaustive, just an attempt to aggregate lesser-known events in one place. Always double-check dates, times and tickets with the organizers or venues before going. This site aggregates external sources and assumes no responsibility for errors, changes or cancellations.
