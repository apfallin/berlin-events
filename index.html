    * Sources (last updated: 12 November 2026)

    Gigs — the Berlin-Gigs community pad, a DIY list of concerts with time, price, genre, smoke-free and wheelchair info.

    MTI — mytrueintent.blogspot.com, a Berlin indie/rock concert blog, imported from its spreadsheet export.

    Mob — mobilize.berlin, a Berlin Mobilizon instance: federated DIY platform for concerts, parties and community events.

    Web — events found on the web on official sites and venue pages; each links directly to its source page.

    Stage — theatre, dance and performance from the theatres’ own calendars across the whole Berlin scene, from the big houses to the freie Szene.

    Art — exhibitions and vernissages from Berlin museums and galleries, discovered via museumsportal.de and berlin.de and verified on official sites.

    Arthouse — special cinema events from Berlin’s independent cinemas and the INDIEKINO aggregator: premieres with guests, festivals and one-off nights.

    Venues — venue addresses and districts verified via web search; the pin next to a venue opens it in OpenStreetMap.

    Events appearing in more than one source are automatically deduplicated (same title + date).

    Thanks — to everyone who maintains these sources and shares event data, and above all to the artists who make Berlin worth going out for. This site only exists because of their work.
